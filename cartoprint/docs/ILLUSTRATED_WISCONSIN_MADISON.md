## Madison prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create original premium pen-and-ink pictorial map of Madison Wisconsin, landscape 4:3, very high resolution. Fine charcoal hand-engraved ink, warm ivory paper, sparse rust-red place labels, intricate little buildings, trees and sailboats; companion aesthetic to reference Tennessee but actual contemporary Madison geography. North-up map composition, Lake Mendota large upper-left/northwest and Lake Monona lower-right/southeast, narrow urban isthmus running southwest to northeast between them. Wisconsin State Capitol dome sits at southwest end of isthmus on square with radiating streets. State Street runs northwest from Capitol to UW campus on southern Mendota shore; Memorial Union Terrace there. Yahara River joins lakes across northeast isthmus. Tree-lined blocks, modest houses, parks, shoreline walking paths, small sailboats. Focus downtown and lake shores, don't invent mountains, castles, giant bridges across lakes, or duplicate capitols. Hand-letter ONLY these accurate labels: Lake Mendota, Lake Monona, Capitol Square, State Street, University of Wisconsin, Yahara River. Keep individual words legible. No large Madison heading, no frame, no legend, no watermark. Generous paper edge for integration into customizable print. The reference is visual aesthetic only; do not reuse Tennessee landform or names.
